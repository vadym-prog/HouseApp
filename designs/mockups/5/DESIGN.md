## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, neo-modernist digital atelier aesthetic for an on-demand household and outdoor service ecosystem. Blending the structural discipline of modern product design tools with the natural vibrancy of home and garden craftsmanship, the aesthetic delivers an atmosphere of instant competence, technological transparency, and effortless reliability.

The visual style is characterized by:
- **Sharp Structural Boundaries:** Ultra-crisp outlines, modular card grids, and fine hairline borders that organize dense service metadata with architectural clarity.
- **Vibrant Functional Accents:** High-energy electric violet and vivid mint/emerald accents that infuse life into utility flows while maintaining a premium software feel.
- **Neo-Modernist Utility:** Clean typography, compact information hierarchy, tactile pill badges, and refined micro-surfaces tailored for rapid, single-handed mobile interactions.
- **Tactile Trust:** Micro-interactions and state transitions feel tactile and definitive, assuring the homeowner that services are transparently priced, tracked in real time, and executed to high standards.

## Colors

The color palette pairs neutral surfaces with saturated, luminous chromatic tokens reminiscent of precision software canvas interfaces:

- **Primary (`#0D99FF` / Electric Cyan-Blue):** Drives global action triggers, verified provider tags, and primary progression controls.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` / Vivid Garden Emerald):** Signifies outdoor, lawn, and gardening categories, successful bookings, active statuses, and ecological sustainability badges.
- **Tertiary (`#7B61FF` / Royal Electric Violet):** Highlights premium artisan tier services, bespoke architectural repairs, and promotional loyalty tiers.
- **Neutral (`#18181B` / Deep Onyx):** Establishes high-contrast text hierarchies, structural hairlines, and solid dark pill accents.

### Role & Status Colors
- **Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Urgent master arrivals, active timers, rating metrics, and caution warnings.
- **Rose (`#F43F5E`):** Cancellation triggers, critical alerts, and disputed services.
- **Subtle Canvas (`#F9FAFB`):** Background foundation creating quiet contrast against bright white foreground cards.
- **Border Gradients:** Reserved for top-tier master highlights and promotional hero banners using an electric linear gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #7B61FF 0%, #0D99FF 100%)`).

## Typography

Typography balances geometric grotesk personality with rigorous technical clarity. The primary typographic voice is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its tight apertures, contemporary humanist curves, and high legibility in both Ukrainian Cyrillic and Latin alphabets. 

**JetBrains Mono** serves as an auxiliary technical accent for pricing tables, timestamps, order identifiers, tracking coordinates, and metric badges, reinforcing an ethos of automated precision and real-time computation.

Key guidelines:
- Apply tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on display and headline weights to deliver a cohesive poster-like editorial lockup.
- Ensure Cyrillic accents (such as ї, є, і, ґ) retain vertical line-height integrity; never set line-heights below `1.2x` on mobile views.
- Restrict monospace numerals to numerical indicators, order IDs, and status timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout system operates on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt micro-subgrid for internal pill and button paddings. Designed primarily for mobile web screens, the interface balances maximum usable thumb zones with disciplined negative space:

- **Mobile Viewports (<640px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins and 12px gutters. All interactive sticky action bars observe a 16px floating offset above the device safe area.
- **Tablet / Expanded Mobile (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins and 16px gutters, docking auxiliary booking summaries into side-drawers.
- **Desktop Dashboard (>1024px):** 12-column constrained fluid grid capped at 1200px container width, centered with automatic margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Modular components stack with `space-md` (12px) separation within groups, and `space-xl` (32px) between distinct category clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows in favor of **crisp structural borders, subtle surface offsets, and directional micro-diffusions**:

- **Ground Plane (Level 0):** Pure or tinted background canvas (`#F9FAFB`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Base Cards & Modules):** Pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`), delimited by a 1px solid hairline border (`#E4E4E7`). No shadow required under normal lighting, creating a calm, flat plane.
- **Interactive Hover & Active Card (Level 2):** Retains 1px hairline border, shifting tone to `#18181B` with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Sheets (Level 3):** Frosted ambient backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with semi-transparent base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), bounded by a top 1px border (`rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`) and high-diffuse elevation: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.12)`.
- **Active State Depths:** When tapped or pressed, cards and primary buttons translate down 1px with zero elevation blur to produce a crisp mechanical response.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified structural curvature:
- **Base Components:** Radius set to `0.5rem` (8px) for input fields, micro-cards, and tooltips.
- **Containers & Sheet Cards (`rounded-lg`):** Set to `1rem` (16px) for service cards, category tiles, master profile sheets, and modal views.
- **Featured Hero Wrappers (`rounded-xl`):** Set to `1.5rem` (24px) for prominent campaign banners and primary map overlays.
- **Pills & Indicator Badges:** Full circular curvature (`9999px`) across all notification chips, filter triggers, status indicators, and micro-avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid onyx background (`#18181B`) with high-contrast white typography (`#FFFFFF`), or electric primary blue (`#0D99FF`). Border radius 12px, vertical padding 12px, horizontal padding 20px. Features subtle 1px inner highlight border. Active state scales to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Action:** White background with 1px border (`#E4E4E7`), dark text (`#18181B`). Hover triggers a 1px border shift to `#18181B`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent background, dark text, hover displays tinted slate (`#F4F4F5`).

### Pill Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Monospace or heavy grotesk labels inside 24px-tall fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  - *Active / On the Way:* Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#059669`), 1px border (`#A7F3D0`), with a pulsing green dot.
  - *Diagnostic Needed:* Amber tint (`#FFFBEB`), amber text (`#D97706`), 1px border (`#FDE68A`).
  - *Urgent Master:* Violet tint (`#EEF2FF`), violet text (`#4F46E5`), 1px border (`#C7D2FE`).
- **Filter Tags:** Toggleable pills with 1px hairline borders (`#E4E4E7`) that invert to solid black (`#18181B`) with white text upon selection.

### Service & Master Cards
- **Construction:** Structured as white monolithic surfaces bounded by 1px neutral borders (`#E4E4E7`).
- **Header:** Category icon housed in a soft square tinted container (`#F4F4F5`), flanked by verified master badges and Ukrainian currency rating counters (`₴ / год`).
- **Footer:** Real-time distance and estimated arrival chips using `label-mono-sm`.

### Inputs & Search
- **Search Bar:** Height 48px, rounded 12px with 1px hairline border (`#E4E4E7`). Prefix features minimal vector search loupe; suffix displays quick-filter activation icon or keyboard shortcut badge in monospace.
- **Form Controls:** Text fields sit on `#FAFAFA` when resting, shifting to `#FFFFFF` with an active 1.5px `#0D99FF` border upon focus. Helper errors render in `#F43F5E` with instant inline shake micro-animation.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Design:** Geometric squares with 4px border radius for checkboxes; circular rings for radio buttons.
- **Selected State:** Solid fill (`#0D99FF` or `#18181B`) housing a clean white vector check icon; transitions employ snappy 150ms spring easing.

### Dynamic Booking Drawer
- Bottom-docked mobile sheet featuring drag indicator pill (`36px x 4px`, `#D4D4D8`). Contains master live tracking, dynamic timeline points, and an instantaneous call/chat trigger lockup.
